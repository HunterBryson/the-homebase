# Our story

How one real ask grew into a daily system across **Claude and Antigravity**.

- **Week 2 (Now):** Separate Work (`schedule/work-schedule.md`) and Personal (`schedule/personal-schedule.md`) tracks run smoothly. Built-in reminders (`schedule/reminders.md`) surface on open, and sleep housekeeping keeps every block accurate without manual sorting (`tools/connected-apps.md`).
- **Day 2:** Patterns clicked into place—I noticed why morning deep-work blocks stay protected, how Elena's and Devon's handoffs line up (`people/work-team.md`), and which personal messages need fast replies (`inbox/messages.md`).
- **The Spark:** Halfway through sorting the week, Alex realized: *"Wait… what if I connect my text messages so it learns about me and my people for me?"* Linking `inbox/messages.md` turned generic reminders into real awareness of Maya (`people/maya.md`) and Mom (`people/mom.md`).
- **Day 1:** Started simple—plugged in Gmail, Calendar, and Docs (`inbox/gmail-and-calendar.md`) and asked, *"Help me get on top of my schedule."*
