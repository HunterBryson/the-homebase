# Desk

The map of my shelves. A topic is one block, called by one word. When Alex says the word—or anything close to it—I read that whole block before I answer.

## Words and blocks

| Word | What it pulls | Block |
|---|---|---|
| where we left off | the whiteboard: where today stands and what's next | `FOOTING.md` |
| about you | Alex's story, priorities, and how we work together | `human/human.md` |
| persona | who I am and how I keep things organized | `self/persona.md` |
| memory review | Alex's ⚑ flags in Homebase, and fixing each one | `self/memory-review.md` |
| our story | how Day 1 grew into Week 2, told by date | `self/story.md` |
| work schedule | deep-work mornings, team check-ins, and weekly pacing | `schedule/work-schedule.md` |
| personal schedule | evenings, gym nights, dinners, and weekend plans | `schedule/personal-schedule.md` |
| reminders | built-in reminders that surface on chat open | `schedule/reminders.md` |
| inbox | Gmail, Google Calendar, and Docs checked at wake | `inbox/gmail-and-calendar.md` |
| messages | text messages connected so I learn who matters | `inbox/messages.md` |
| Maya | Alex's close friend—dinner plans and weekend hikes | `people/maya.md` |
| Mom | Sunday calls, family plans, and gift ideas | `people/mom.md` |
| work team | Elena, Devon, and Priya on shared weekly projects | `people/work-team.md` |
| connected apps | what's plugged in across Claude and Antigravity | `tools/connected-apps.md` |

## Walking it
- Saving is a pull in reverse: what matters goes straight into the block its word opens in the same turn.
- Work (`schedule/work-schedule.md`) and Personal (`schedule/personal-schedule.md`) stay in separate blocks so neither crowds the other.
- A new person or recurring topic gets its own block and a row here without asking Alex to organize anything.
